PlanetRead uses [Same Language Subtitling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same_language_subtitling) to build reading skills: subtitles in the same language as the song or film. Aligning those subtitles by hand is slow. These plugins let an editor click or slide subtitle timings into place inside Premiere Pro.
